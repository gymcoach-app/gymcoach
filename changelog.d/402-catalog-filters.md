Browse the exercise catalog by muscle and by gym equipment: tap a muscle on
the body silhouette (or pick it in the equivalent keyboard and screen-reader
list) and switch on "Only what my gym has" to keep the exercises a saved gym
can offer. Both combine with the search, and an empty result offers one
"Clear filters" action. Bodyweight exercises and exercises with no set
equipment type stay listed, and a gym with no saved equipment says so instead
of showing a bare short list (#402).
