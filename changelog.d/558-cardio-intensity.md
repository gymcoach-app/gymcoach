A cardio exercise in a program can carry an intensity target next to its
duration: an RPE from 1 to 10 and a heart rate range in bpm, both optional.
The workout card and the print sheet read "20 min · RPE 6 · 140-150 bpm", the
live session shows the intensity to hold in the set logger next to its inputs,
the coach reads it as the plan, and backups carry it (format version 15; older
backups still import). Second slice of the cardio prescription asked for by
mattordev (#558).
